# Childcare voucher in Remerbaach

## What the voucher covers

The childcare voucher reduces the hourly rate charged by approved childcare
providers in the commune, including the crèche, the maison relais, and
approved childminders. The reduction depends on the household income and on
the number of children in the household, and it is applied by the provider
rather than paid to the parent.

The voucher covers a maximum of sixty hours per week per child during term
time and a maximum of fifty hours per week during school holidays. Hours
beyond the maximum are charged at the provider's full rate. Meals are
covered at a fixed rate per meal and are counted separately from the hourly
allowance.

The voucher does not cover activities organised outside the approved
providers, including private music lessons, sports clubs, and holiday camps
run by associations. The commune runs a separate and much smaller subsidy
for those, decided case by case by the social office.

## Who can apply

Any household with at least one child under thirteen and a main residence in
the commune. The child must be registered in the population register at the
same address as the applicant. Households where the child alternates between
two addresses apply at the address where the child is registered, and the
other parent may not apply separately for the same child.

Secondary residents are not eligible. Families who move into the commune
during the year can apply from the date of the arrival declaration, and the
rate is applied from the first day of the following month.

## Documents required

The application requires a completed application form, a residence
certificate, the birth certificate of each child, and proof of household
income. Proof of income means the most recent tax assessment for both
parents, or three consecutive payslips where no tax assessment exists, or an
attestation from the social office for households with no taxable income.

Self-employed applicants provide the most recent tax assessment and, where
the assessment is more than eighteen months old, a statement from their
accountant. Households in which one parent works abroad provide the foreign
income statement together with a translation, which does not have to be
sworn.

Where a document is missing, the social office issues a provisional rate at
the least favourable band, corrects it once the file is complete, and
reimburses the difference through the provider.

## Where to apply

Applications are submitted at the communal social office, which is open on
Tuesday and Thursday from 08:30 to 11:30. An appointment is recommended and
can be made by telephone or through the communal website. The office
confirms the applicable rate in writing within fifteen working days.

The social office also advises on the national allowances that exist
alongside the communal voucher, and it can forward a file to the Ministry on
the applicant's behalf. It cannot decide a national allowance, and residents
are sometimes surprised by that boundary.

## Renewal

The voucher is valid for twelve months and must be renewed with fresh proof
of income. A change of household income of more than ten per cent must be
reported within one month, and the rate is adjusted from the following
month. A change that is not reported and is discovered later leads to a
recovery of the difference over the whole period concerned.

A renewal application submitted more than four weeks before the expiry date
is processed in time for a seamless transition. A renewal submitted late
leaves a gap during which the provider charges the full rate, and the gap is
not reimbursed.
