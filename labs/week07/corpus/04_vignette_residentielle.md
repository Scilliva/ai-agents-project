# Resident parking vignette, 2026 scheme

## Who can apply

Any resident registered at an address inside one of the four controlled
parking zones may apply for a resident parking vignette. A household may
hold at most two vignettes. The vehicle must be registered at the
applicant's address or accompanied by a company vehicle attestation signed
by the employer and dated within the last three months.

Secondary residents may apply for one vignette only, and only in zones C and
D. Students registered as main residents have the same rights as any other
main resident. A resident who does not own a vehicle may not hold a vignette
in reserve, and a vignette is cancelled when the vehicle it names is sold.

Leased and long-term rental vehicles qualify on production of the lease
agreement. Vehicles registered to a person who is not a member of the
household do not qualify, including a vehicle belonging to a parent living
elsewhere.

## How to apply

Applications are made on form RB-1140b, available at the reception desk and
on the communal website. The completed form RB-1140b must be submitted with
a residence certificate issued in the last three months and a copy of the
vehicle registration document. Applications are processed within ten working
days and the vignette is sent by post.

An application may be submitted by post or in person. It may not currently
be submitted by electronic mail, because the form requires a signature and
the commune has not yet joined the national electronic signature scheme.
Incomplete applications are returned with a note listing what is missing,
and the ten working days start again from the day the complete file arrives.

A change of vehicle during the year is handled as a modification rather than
a new application. The resident returns the old vignette with a short
written note and receives a replacement free of charge.

## Fees

The resident parking vignette costs 60 euros per year for the first vehicle
of a household and 120 euros per year for the second vehicle. The fee is not
prorated and the vignette expires on 31 December regardless of the issue
date. Replacement of a lost vignette costs 15 euros.

Payment is made by bank transfer on receipt of the invoice, which is sent
with the vignette. Payment in cash at the reception desk is possible for
amounts under 100 euros. An unpaid vignette is cancelled after two reminders
and the cancellation is notified to the parking enforcement service.

Households in receipt of the communal social allowance pay a reduced fee of
20 euros for the first vehicle. The reduction is applied automatically where
the social office has confirmed eligibility, and it is not applied
retroactively.

## Zones

Zone A covers the village centre, zone B the station quarter, zone C the
Belval road, and zone D the industrial park. A vignette is valid in the zone
printed on it and in no other zone. The zone is determined by the address on
the residence certificate and cannot be chosen by the applicant.

Parking in a controlled zone without a valid vignette is enforced between
08:00 and 19:00 on Monday to Saturday. Outside those hours all four zones
are free for any vehicle. Zone D is additionally free during the whole of
August.

## Visitors

Households in zones A and B may buy up to twenty visitor day passes per year
at 2 euros each, on the same form RB-1140b, by ticking the visitor box. The
passes are issued as a booklet and the resident writes the date and the
registration number on the pass before it is displayed.

Passes are not transferable between households and unused passes are not
refunded at the end of the year. Households in zones C and D do not receive
visitor passes, because parking pressure in those zones is judged
sufficiently low.
