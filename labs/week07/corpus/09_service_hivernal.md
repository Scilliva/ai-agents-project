# Winter service in Remerbaach

## What the commune clears

The communal technical service clears and grits the communal roads, the
approaches to the school and the health centre, and the shuttle stops. Roads
are treated in a priority order published each November: first the bus
routes and the school approaches, then the collector roads, then the
residential streets. In a heavy snowfall the third category can wait until
the following day, and the commune does not undertake to clear it overnight.

The national roads crossing the commune are cleared by the national roads
administration and not by the commune. Residents reporting an untreated
national road to the communal service will be redirected, which is a
frequent source of frustration and is stated here to save a telephone call.

Private roads and shared driveways are the responsibility of their owners.
The commune will not enter a private road even where it serves several
households.

## Obligations of residents

Residents, or the owner where the dwelling is rented, must clear the
pavement in front of their property over a width of at least one and a half
metres, between 06:00 and 20:00, and must remove ice. Cleared snow may not
be pushed onto the carriageway or onto a cycle lane. The obligation applies
on Sundays and public holidays.

Where a rental contract places the obligation on the tenant, the commune
still addresses the owner, because the commune is not a party to the
contract. Owners of an unoccupied property remain fully responsible. In a
building with several dwellings the obligation falls on the syndic, or in
the absence of a syndic on all the owners jointly.

Salt may be used on public pavements. The commune asks residents to prefer
grit where a pavement borders a planted verge, because salt run-off damages
the verge and the trees planted in it. There is no penalty attached to this
request.

## Grit bins

Grit bins are placed at twenty-two locations from mid-November to
mid-March. The grit is for public pavements and roads and may not be used on
private driveways. The bins are refilled on request and, in a long cold
spell, twice a week without a request.

The locations are published on the communal website with a map. Residents
may propose a new location before the end of September for the following
winter, and the technical service decides on the basis of the gradient and
the number of households served.

## Reporting a problem

An untreated road can be reported to the technical service by telephone
during working hours and through the communal website at any time. Reports
received outside working hours are treated on the next working morning,
except for reports concerning a bus route or the health centre approach,
which are forwarded to the standby team.

A report should give the street, the nearest house number, and the time of
the observation. Reports that give only a street name are still acted on but
take longer, because the crew has to find the section concerned.
