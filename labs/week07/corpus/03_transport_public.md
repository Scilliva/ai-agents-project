# Public transport in Remerbaach

## The communal shuttle

The commune operates a small shuttle, the Bummelbus Remerbaach, connecting
the three villages of the commune with the railway station and the health
centre. The shuttle runs every forty minutes between 06:20 and 20:00 on
weekdays and hourly on Saturdays. There is no service on Sundays or public
holidays, and the service is reduced to hourly during the school summer
holidays.

The route has fourteen stops. Nine of them are served in both directions and
five are served only on the outward run, which is a source of confusion and
is marked on the printed timetable with an arrow. The vehicle can be stopped
at any point along the route between two marked stops if the driver judges
it safe, a practice the commune calls the free stop and which does not apply
on the section along the national road.

## Fares

Travel on the communal shuttle is free of charge for all passengers. No
ticket and no registration are required, and there is no distinction between
residents and visitors. The shuttle is not part of the regional network and
its timetable is published separately by the communal technical service.

Because the shuttle is free, it carries no ticketing equipment and no
passenger counting system. The commune estimates use twice a year through a
manual count over one week, which is why the published passenger figures
should be read as an order of magnitude rather than as a measurement.

Passengers continuing onto the regional network pay the regional fare from
the station in the ordinary way. The commune does not issue combined
tickets.

## Accessibility

Two of the four shuttle vehicles are low floor and can carry one wheelchair.
Passengers who need the accessible vehicle can reserve a seat by telephone
until 17:00 on the previous working day. The reservation is for a specific
departure and the vehicle waits up to three minutes at the stop.

Guide dogs and assistance dogs travel free and without a reservation. Other
dogs travel on a lead and at the driver's discretion, and are not permitted
when the vehicle is full. Pushchairs must be folded when the wheelchair
space is occupied.

## School transport

School transport for the communal primary school is organised separately and
is reserved for enrolled pupils. Parents receive the route and the stop list
with the enrolment confirmation in July. Pupils in the first two years are
handed over only to a named adult, and the list of named adults is set at
enrolment and can be changed in writing at any time.

The school transport does not run during school holidays. Families needing
transport during the holiday care weeks use the ordinary communal shuttle,
which does not stop at the school and stops instead at the sports hall two
hundred metres away.
