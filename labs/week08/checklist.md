# Week 8 checklist

## Checkpoint 1, it survives the harness

Six items. Do not move to block 3 until all six are true.

- [ ] The unhandled baseline is recorded: for each of the eight faults, what
      the pipeline did **before** you fixed anything
- [ ] `is_retryable` draws a line you can defend, and contract drift is on
      the far side of it
- [ ] Backoff is exponential with jitter, capped in attempts **and** in
      total elapsed time
- [ ] Contract drift is caught by validation rather than by a retry, and
      your `fees` step reports **one** attempt on that fault, not three
- [ ] The degraded answer names what is missing in the text the user reads,
      not only in a log
- [ ] You can say what your pipeline does with an empty result and why

**If your fees step retries three times on the drift fault**, `is_retryable`
is wrong. The call succeeded.

## Checkpoint 2, the gate holds

Six items. Be ready to demonstrate the resume.

- [ ] Exactly one step is gated, and you can say in a sentence why the
      others are not
- [ ] The run stops at the gate, the process exits, and a **second** process
      resumes it from the checkpoint
- [ ] The approval payload carries the exact arguments and the evidence, and
      no text generated by a model
- [ ] Reject and edit both work, and an edited approval submits the **edited**
      arguments rather than the original
- [ ] The decision record holds the verdict, an identity, and what changed,
      and it survives the process
- [ ] You have put a side effect above the interrupt, run both processes,
      and checked whether the state shows it twice

The last item is the one to actually do rather than read. It executes twice
and appears once.

## Before you leave

- [ ] Code committed and pushed
- [ ] `python -m project.verify` passes
- [ ] `DECISIONS.md` has today's four decisions
- [ ] The stretch attempted, or explicitly deferred with a reason

## Homework, before week 9

- [ ] The timeout on an unanswered approval, with proof that it fires
- [ ] One trace where a retry, a fallback, and the gate are all visible
- [ ] One sentence on what your fault harness cannot simulate

## What "done" means here

The deliverable is the four decisions and the two-process demonstration. A
student whose pipeline survives every fault and who cannot say which step is
gated and why has not done the session. A student who can explain why
hardening produced more degraded answers rather than more correct ones has
understood it.
