# Communal fees and taxes in Remerbaach

## Overview

The commune levies an annual waste fee, an annual water and sanitation
charge, a dog tax, and a set of administrative fees. Invoices are issued
twice a year, in March and in September, and are payable within thirty days.
The rates are fixed by the communal council each December for the following
year and are published in the communal bulletin.

Invoices are sent to the address in the population register. A resident who
has moved without declaring the departure continues to receive invoices at
the old address, which is the single most common cause of a reminder charge.

## Dog tax

Every dog older than four months must be declared to the communal
administration. The declaration deadline is 31 January of each year, or
within one month of acquiring the dog if that is later. The tax is due for
the whole year regardless of the month of declaration, and it is not
refunded if the dog dies or is rehomed during the year.

Assistance dogs and dogs belonging to a registered rescue association are
exempt on production of the relevant attestation. A dog kept for fewer than
three months by a foster carer working with an approved association is also
exempt, and the exemption must be requested in advance rather than claimed
afterwards.

The declaration records the dog's identification number, its breed group,
and the third party liability insurance policy, which is compulsory. A dog
declared without an insurance reference is registered and the owner is asked
to supply the reference within one month.

## Payment difficulties

Residents who cannot pay within thirty days may request a payment plan from
the communal treasury before the due date. Requests made after a reminder
has been issued carry an administrative charge of 12 euros. A payment plan
may spread an invoice over up to six monthly instalments and carries no
interest.

The treasury will not agree a second payment plan while a first is running.
Households in persistent difficulty are referred to the social office, which
can in some cases assume the waste and water charges directly. That referral
is confidential and is not recorded on the tax file.
