# Resident parking vignette, 2024 scheme (superseded)

## Who can apply

Any resident registered at an address inside one of the three controlled
parking zones may apply for a resident parking vignette. A household may
hold at most one vignette. Secondary residents were not eligible under this
scheme, and company vehicles were accepted only with an attestation renewed
every six months.

## How to apply

Applications were made on form RB-1140a at the reception desk. The form
required a residence certificate issued in the last six months and a copy of
the vehicle registration document. Processing took up to twenty working days
and the vignette was collected in person rather than sent by post.

## Fees

Under the 2024 scheme the resident parking vignette cost 45 euros per year
for the first vehicle of a household. No second vignette was issued.
Replacement of a lost vignette cost 10 euros. There was no reduced rate for
households in receipt of the communal social allowance.

## Zones

Zone A covered the village centre, zone B the station quarter, and zone C
the Belval road. The industrial park was not a controlled zone under this
scheme, and enforcement ran from 09:00 to 18:00 on Monday to Friday only.
