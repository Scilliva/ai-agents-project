# Waste collection and recycling in Remerbaach

## Bins issued to each household

Each household receives four bins: a grey bin for residual waste, a blue bin
for paper and cardboard, a brown bin for organic kitchen waste, and a green
bin for garden clippings. The standard grey bin holds one hundred and twenty
litres. Additional or larger bins can be requested at the technical service
and change the annual waste fee, which is set out on the communal fees page.

Bins are delivered within ten working days of an arrival declaration and
remain the property of the commune. A damaged bin is replaced free of charge
where the damage occurred during collection, and at the resident's expense
otherwise. A lost bin is replaced once free of charge per household per five
years.

Bins must be presented at the kerb before 06:00 on the collection day and
removed from the public pavement by 20:00 on the same day. A bin that is
overfilled so that the lid does not close is not emptied, and the crew
leaves a written notice explaining why.

## Collection calendar

The grey bin is collected every second Tuesday. The blue bin is collected on
the first Thursday of the month. The brown bin is collected every Monday
between April and October, and every second Monday between November and
March. The full calendar for the year is delivered to every household in
December and is published on the communal website.

Where a collection day falls on a public holiday, the collection moves to
the next working day and every later collection in that week moves by one
day as well. Residents are advised not to rely on the printed calendar in
the weeks around Christmas, Easter, and the national holiday.

The three villages of the commune are collected on the same days but by
different vehicles, so a delay in one village does not mean the collection
has been cancelled in another.

## Green waste

Garden clippings, grass, hedge cuttings, and leaves go in the green bin. The
green bin is collected on the third Wednesday of the month from March to
November inclusive, and it is not collected in December, January, or
February. Residents with a large garden may request a second green bin at no
additional charge, which is the only bin exempt from the additional bin fee.

Branches thicker than eight centimetres are not accepted in the green bin
and must be taken to the recycling centre. Soil, stones, and turf are not
green waste and are refused at both the kerb and the recycling centre.
Treated wood, painted wood, and wood containing nails are handled as bulky
waste.

Christmas trees are collected separately in the second week of January, from
the kerb, without decorations and without the stand.

## Recycling centre

The recycling centre on route de Belval accepts glass, metal, electronics,
paint, solvents, batteries, textiles, and bulky items. It is open Tuesday to
Saturday from 09:00 to 17:00 and closed on public holidays. Residents must
present a valid residence certificate on entry, and vehicles with a trailer
are directed to the second gate.

Quantities are limited to two cubic metres per household per visit and ten
cubic metres per household per year. Construction and demolition waste is
accepted only in small quantities and only from private households, never
from a business. Businesses registered in the commune have a separate
contract and a separate access card.

## Bulky waste on request

Bulky items can also be collected from the kerb twice a year on request.
Requests are made by telephone or through the communal website at least ten
working days before the collection date. The request must list the items,
because the crew refuses anything not on the list.

Items must be placed at the kerb on the evening before the collection and
must not obstruct the pavement or a cycle lane. Electrical appliances
containing refrigerant are collected separately and must be declared as such
when the request is made.
